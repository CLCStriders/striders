#### Birmingham and Midlands League cross country

The weekend saw the first round of the Birmingham League cross country competition. Both the men’s and women’s CLC Striders teams are in the first division for this season, competing with some of the best clubs in the region. The ladies were first to race on Saturday afternoon on a four mile course around Abington Park Northampton. First to finish for the Striders was Kate Telford in 66th place in a time of 24:50. She was closely followed by Helen Knight in 71st and a time of 25:03. Liza Bradshaw finished in 116th in 27:13, with Rachel Vines in 147th in 28:56, and Amelia Mullins in 154th in a time of 29:19. The ladies finished in 20th place overall, just ahead of local rivals, Cheltenham and County Harriers. However, in the master’s competition, they place first, setting an excellent precedent for the rest of the season.

The men raced on a 6 mile course, completing four laps of Abington Park in the waning sunshine. First to finish for the Striders was the ever reliable Pete Duffy in 68th place overall and a time of 34:28. A cluster of Striders finished fairly close together in 121, 124, 126 and 128; led by Tom Burgess in 37:19, Tom Fletcher in 37:24, Gavin Woods in 37:31 and Izaac Cole in 37:33.  Dan Bazzard finished in 171st in a time of 40:35, with Nick Ledwold in 176th and 41:11, Lee Ormerod in 188th and 42:30, and Adam Greenwood in 193rd with a time of 42:57. Duffy, Burgess, Fletcher, Woods, Cole and Bazzard were the six to score for the team, resulting in 15th place overall. In the master’s competition they came 12th.

#### Broadway Marathon

The weekend racing continued on Sunday with four Striders taking on the Broadway Marathon which loops up and down on the Cotswold Escarpment. Richard Elsworthy returned as the defending champion and did not disappoint. He led from start to finish and completed the course in a time of 3:18:31, seven minutes clear of second place. Next to finish was James Endicott in 15th place and a time of 4:05:02. He was followed by Tom fletcher doing his second race of the weekend, who finished in  35th in a time of 4:36:24. It was arguably Tom’s third race of the weekend, as he also tore round the Cheltenham Parkrun course on Saturday morning. Completing the club quartet was Gavin Jerman who finished in 60th place overall in a time of 4:56:43, and first place in the male 60-65 category. It was a testing day in the Cotswold with the rain bucketing down in the latter part of the race, turning some parts of the course into rivers.

#### Callow Fell Race

Also racing off-road this weekend was Ben Wade in the Callow Fell Race in Shropshire. Tackling a 5 mile course with 762 m of ascent, Wade finished in 21st place overall in a time of 1:11:26.

#### Ridgeway Ultra-Adventure

Finally, one Strider started his event before the weekend commenced. Rob Bircher completed the three day, 83 mile, Ridgeway Ultra-Adventure. Averaging more than a marathon a day, it was a fantastic achievement.
