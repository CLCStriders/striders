We're delighted to welcome a 6th club coach to our roster as Kate Telford successfully completes her Coach in Running Fitness qualification with England Athletics. 

Kate joins Amelia, Jamie, Joel, Jon and Matt as the club's qualified coaches. 

Kate brings a wealth of experience to the role having competed on the track, road, trail and cross country to great success; winning multiple races, team awards and county medals on the way. She is also a 6 star finisher of the World Marathon Majors and a SuperHalfs Super Medallist

Well done Kate and thanks for your commitment to our wonderful club!
