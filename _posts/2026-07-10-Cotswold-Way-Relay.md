The 2026 Cotswold Way Relay saw an outstanding showcase of grit, teamwork, and stellar individual performances from the CLC Striders across all ten demanding legs of the historic trail. Fielding three competitive squads - the all male Trail Titans, the mixed Limestone Legends, and the all female Summit Striders - the club battled the heat, steep climbs, rolling terrains, and intense competition to deliver a memorable day of racing.

#### Leg 1: Chipping Campden to Stanway House (12 Miles)
The relay kicked off with a grueling 12-mile opening leg. Leading the charge for the club was Pete Duffy of the Trail Titans, who produced a masterclass performance to finish a superb 3rd overall out of 103 runners in a blistering time of 1:22:47. Tom Fletcher spearheaded the Limestone Legends’ campaign with a very strong run, crossing the line in 1:31:15 to claim 19th position. Completing the Leg 1 trio, Kate Telford fought hard for the Summit Striders, securing 46th place with a solid time of 1:47:31.

#### Leg 2: Stanway House to Cleeve Common (11.8 Miles)
Leg 2 presented 11.8 miles of punishing ascents. The Trail Titans’ Oli Wright-Jones proved to be the ultimate 'super sub', stepping in at short notice to clock an impressive 1:45:45 and finish 27th. For the Summit Striders, Alice Johnston put together a highly determined performance to take 63rd place in 1:58:42. Meanwhile, Emma Abbey showed great resolve over the tough terrain for the Limestone Legends, placing 92nd in 2:20:19.

#### Leg 3: Cleeve Common to Crickley Hill (13.5 Miles)
The longest stage of the relay at 13.5 miles and with the greatest ascent (1,988 ft) to boot, the revised leg 3 was described by race organisers as ‘a monster’’. Peter Mitchener of the Trail Titans handled the distance with aplomb, finishing 64th in 2:11:12. His incredible dedication was duly recognised as he was awarded the prestigious title of 'King of the Cotswolds' for the extraordinary feat of having now run all 10 individual legs of the Cotswold Way over his racing career. Laura Leaver executed a fine, disciplined race for the Summit Striders to capture 86th place in 2:28:19. Unfortunately, the Limestone Legends’ Ed Collier fell victim to the unforgiving stage and missed the strict time cutoff.

#### Leg 4: Crickley Hill to Painswick (10.3 Miles)
Nigel Bailey dug in deep for the Trail Titans, bringing the baton home in 1:46:08 to secure 67th place. Nicola Weager ran a close and well-paced race right behind him for the Summit Striders, finishing just over a minute later in 1:47:13 for 70th position. The Limestone Legends faced further misfortune on this leg as Ed Thompson missed the time cutoff.

#### Leg 5: Painswick to Ebley Mill (9.3 Miles)
The 9.3-mile fifth leg sparked a thrilling set of performances, particularly in the women's category. Scarlett Bell stole the headlines for the Summit Striders, flying through the course in 1:13:24 to finish a sensational 16th overall and cross the line as the 3rd placed female runner. Alex Doherty ran brilliantly to lead the Trail Titans' effort, taking 32nd place in a swift 1:22:31. Not far behind, James Babbage delivered a superb performance for the Limestone Legends, finishing 45th in 1:27:00.

#### Leg 6: Dudbridge to Dursley (8.7 Miles)
Over the 8.7 miles of Leg 6, the female athletes continued to dominate the spotlight. Helen Knight produced an exceptional run for the Summit Striders, navigating the course in 1:19:49 to finish 18th overall and claim a magnificent 2nd place in the female standings. Luke Hunter kept the momentum high for the Trail Titans, executing a very strong race to finish 37th in 1:28:51. Philip Abbey also put in a stellar shift for the Limestone Legends, finishing a highly respectable 51st with a time of 1:35:24.

#### Leg 7: Dursley to Wotton Under Edge (7.5 Miles)
Leg 7 offered the shortest distance of the day at 7.5 miles, but it lacked nothing in intensity. Lee Ormerod showed excellent speed for the Trail Titans, leading the club's contingent home in 1:08:48 to take 34th place. Just one minute later, Sophie Longmore crossed the line for the Summit Striders in 1:09:48, earning a brilliant 38th overall and securing a spot as the 5th placed female runner. Liam Richardson rounded off a highly competitive leg for the club, clocking 1:11:41 to finish 44th for the Limestone Legends.

#### Leg 8: Wotton Under Edge to Old Sodbury (12.1 Miles)
The second longest leg stretched over 12.1 miles and produced arguably the Striders’ standout performance of the entire relay. Ellie Pudifoot of the Summit Striders delivered a masterclass, conquering the course in 1:41:15 to finish 14th overall. Her incredible run earned her the title of 1st female on the leg. Queen of the Cotswolds Liza Bradshaw ran a superb race to anchor the Limestone Legends' revival, placing 30th in 1:49:46. For the Trail Titans, Jamie Hartnoll kept his composure, finishing close behind in 33rd place with a time of 1:53:12.

#### Leg 9: Old Sodbury to Cold Ashton (9.3 Miles)
With the finish line nearing, the 9.3 miles of Leg 9 saw the Striders turn the screw. John Comer ran a solid race for the Trail Titans, storming through to finish 15th overall in a rapid 1:12:41. He was pushed all the way by Karlien Heyrman of the Summit Striders, who finished a mere 24 seconds later in 1:13:05, securing 17th overall and a brilliant 4th place female finish. Gavin Jerman put in a highly commendable shift for the Limestone Legends, finishing 54th in 1:28:01.

#### Leg 10: Cold Ashton to Bath Abbey (9.8 Miles)
The final 9.8-mile leg brought the runners down into the historic surroundings of Bath Abbey. Arnie Delstanche provided a spectacular grand finale for the Trail Titans, flying down the trail to finish 11th overall in a superb 1:12:33. Fiona Brady-Miller closed out an incredibly successful day for the Summit Striders, placing 48th in 1:32:35. Bringing the Limestone Legends home to cheers from the traveling support, Colin Upton showed great endurance to finish 94th in 1:54:03.

In the team standings the all male Trail Titans team finished 13th in the open team category in 15 hours 24 with the Limestone Legends 32nd out 43 open teams in total, in 18 hours 22 . The Striders ladies team the Summit Striders finished in an excellent 4th place in the Women’s team competition with a combined time of 16:11:41, the third fastest Striders women’s team performance in the club’s relay history.

All in all, an incredible collective effort from CLC Striders. From Pete Duffy's podium finish start on leg 1 to Ellie Pudifoot's dominant leg 8 victory and Peter Mitchener's coronation as 'King of the Cotswolds', the 2026 relay will live long in the memory. Every single runner played their part on an unforgettable day for the Striders on the trails.
